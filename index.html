Best use of the tool
Use it to compare expected return, premium-hit frequency and how quickly multi-pack purchases increase your chance of at least one premium card. Treat “packs remaining” as context, not a hot-streak indicator. Without a live record of which specific premium or high-value cards have already been removed, there is no defensible way to claim that the final packs have better or worse odds than the published starting pool.
This is a probability and expected-value calculator based on the pack data shown in your screenshots. It does not predict the next card, and published comp values may differ from realised resale prices.
